Barnaby's Story

Rescuer Favorite

Barnaby was found wandering gently along the greenbelt trail after a heavy rainstorm in late autumn. Cold but remarkably sweet, he trotted right up to a trail runner and offered a muddy paw. Since joining Austin Haven, he has become the resident "big brother" to shy rescue pups.

He loves brisk morning strolls, snoozing beside your desk during Zoom calls, and soft squeaky plush toys. His tail begins thumping gently the moment anyone makes eye contact!
